# Procurement Policy

## Raising a purchase request
Any purchase above Rs. 25,000 needs a purchase request in the procurement system before an order is placed. Purchases below Rs. 25,000 may be made with a department card.

## Quotations
Purchases between Rs. 25,000 and Rs. 5,00,000 require at least three written quotations. Purchases above Rs. 5,00,000 require a formal tender run by the procurement team.

## Vendor onboarding
New vendors must provide a valid GSTIN, PAN, cancelled cheque and a signed code of conduct. Vendor GSTINs are verified on the GST portal before the first payment.

## Payment terms
Standard payment terms are 45 days from receipt of a valid GST invoice. Payments to registered MSME suppliers are made within 45 days as required by the MSMED Act.

## Approval limits {access: confidential}
Department heads may approve purchase orders up to Rs. 10,00,000. Orders between Rs. 10,00,000 and Rs. 50,00,000 need CFO approval. Orders above Rs. 50,00,000 need approval from the managing director and the board audit committee.
