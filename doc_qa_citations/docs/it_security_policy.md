# IT Security Policy

## Passwords and multi-factor authentication
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and must not be reused across systems. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, VPN and all finance systems. Passwords must never be shared, including with IT staff.

## Laptops and devices
Company laptops must have full-disk encryption and the approved endpoint protection agent installed. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 2 hours.

## Reporting a security incident
Employees must report suspected phishing emails, malware or data leaks to the IT helpdesk immediately using the Report Phish button or the helpdesk portal. Do not forward suspicious emails to colleagues.

## Use of AI tools
Only company-approved AI assistants may be used for work. Customer personal data, source code and unreleased financial information must not be pasted into public AI tools.

## Incident escalation and regulator reporting {access: confidential}
The security lead classifies incidents within 1 hour of detection. Reportable cyber incidents are notified to CERT-In within 6 hours of being noticed, as required by the CERT-In Directions of April 2022. The incident commander maintains the war-room bridge and contacts the external forensics retainer.

## Privileged access reviews {access: confidential}
Administrator accounts are reviewed every quarter by the IT head. Break-glass admin credentials are stored in the hardware vault and rotated after every use.
