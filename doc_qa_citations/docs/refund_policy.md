# Returns and Refund Policy

## Return window
Most products can be returned within 10 days of delivery. Electronics can be returned within 7 days only if they are defective or damaged on arrival.

## Non-returnable items
Innerwear, perishable groceries, personalised products and opened software cannot be returned.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days after the returned item passes quality check. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer or store credit.

## Cancellations
Orders can be cancelled free of charge before they are shipped. After shipping, the order must be refused at delivery or returned under this policy.

## Damaged or wrong items
If you receive a damaged or wrong item, report it within 48 hours with photos through the app. We will arrange a free pickup and a replacement or full refund.
