# Privacy Notice for Customers

## What personal data we collect
We collect your name, mobile number, email address, delivery address and order history when you shop with Tarangini Retail. We do not collect more data than we need for these purposes.

## Purpose of processing
We process your personal data to fulfil orders, provide customer support, process refunds and, only with your consent, send marketing messages. Each purpose is listed in the consent form you see at sign-up.

## Your consent and withdrawal
Consent is taken through a clear opt-in and is not bundled with other terms. You can withdraw consent at any time from your account settings or by writing to our Grievance Officer, and withdrawal is as easy as giving consent.

## Your rights
You can ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or erase it, and nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity.

## Data retention
We keep order records for 8 years to meet tax law requirements. Marketing data is erased within 30 days after you withdraw consent.

## Grievance Officer
You can contact our Grievance Officer at privacy@tarangini-retail.example. We respond to grievances within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
