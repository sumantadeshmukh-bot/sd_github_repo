# Leave Policy - Tarangini Retail Pvt Ltd (fictional company)

## Scope
This policy applies to all full-time employees of Tarangini Retail. Contract staff follow the leave terms in their contract.

## Earned leave
Employees accrue 18 days of earned leave per calendar year, credited at 1.5 days per month. Up to 30 days of unused earned leave can be carried forward to the next year. Earned leave must be applied for at least 7 days in advance through the HR portal.

## Sick leave
Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days. Unused sick leave does not carry forward.

## Maternity and paternity leave
Eligible women employees receive 26 weeks of paid maternity leave in line with the Maternity Benefit Act. Paternity leave is 10 working days, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption.

## Public holidays
The company observes 10 public holidays each year. The holiday list is published by HR every December for the following year.

## Leave encashment
Unused earned leave above the carry-forward limit is encashed at basic salary at the end of each calendar year. Sick leave cannot be encashed.
