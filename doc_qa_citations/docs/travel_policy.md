# Travel and Expense Policy

## Booking travel
All business travel must be pre-approved by the reporting manager and booked through the company travel desk. Domestic flights must be economy class and booked at least 14 days ahead where possible.

## Hotel limits
Hotel stays are reimbursed up to Rs. 6,000 per night in metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad) and up to Rs. 4,000 per night in other cities. Stays above the limit need written approval from a department head.

## Daily allowance
A daily allowance of Rs. 1,200 covers meals and local incidentals on outstation travel. No separate meal bills are reimbursed when the daily allowance is claimed.

## Local conveyance
Cab rides for client visits are reimbursed at actuals with a GST invoice. Use of a personal car is reimbursed at Rs. 12 per kilometre.

## Expense claims
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of returning from travel, with original GST invoices or receipts. Claims submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed.
